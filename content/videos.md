# Videos

One video per line, newest first, starting with "- " at the very start of the line (the indented line below is only an example):

    - 2026-11-12 | Title of the video | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID

Short links (https://youtu.be/VIDEO_ID) and Shorts links also work.
Lines that do not start with "- " are ignored.
