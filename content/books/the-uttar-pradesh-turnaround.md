How a monk from Gorakhpur took charge of India's most populous state in 2017 and set out to remake it: its law and order, its roads, its investment and its sense of itself.

The book follows what Yogi Adityanath did with Uttar Pradesh after 2017: law and order, expressways, investment, welfare and faith.
