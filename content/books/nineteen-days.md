A narrative account of the nineteen days from the Pahalgam terror attack of 22 April 2025 to the ceasefire of 10 May 2025.

The families, the decisions, the weapons made in Bharat, and the doctrine that came out of nineteen days in 2025.

## The nineteen days

- **22 April:** Terror attack at Pahalgam
- **23 April:** Indus Waters Treaty put in abeyance
- **7 May:** Operation Sindoor strikes nine terror sites
- **10 May:** Ceasefire
