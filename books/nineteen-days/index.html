Nineteen Days

Pahalgam, Operation Sindoor and the Answer of Bharat · by Kamlesh Bhagatkar

Publishers & media: get in touch

On 22 April 2025, terrorists killed twenty-six people on a meadow above Pahalgam. Fifteen days later India struck nine terror sites across the border. On 10 May the firing stopped. Nineteen Days tells the story of the days in between.

It is told through the people who had to live inside the headlines: the families of those killed, the pony men of the meadow, families parted at the Attari gate, the officers who spoke for the operation, and civilians in Poonch under shelling. It moves between the meadow and the briefing room, the hotline and the bunker.

The book is built entirely from the public record: news reports, official statements and published interviews, with endnotes for every chapter. Nothing is invented. Where India and Pakistan disagree, over casualties, aircraft or who brought about the ceasefire, the book says who claimed what.

If you followed those days on your phone and never saw the whole of it, this book puts the nineteen days together in one place.

The nineteen days

  • 22 April: Terror attack at Pahalgam
  • 23 April: Indus Waters Treaty put in abeyance
  • 7 May: Operation Sindoor strikes nine terror sites
  • 10 May: Ceasefire
