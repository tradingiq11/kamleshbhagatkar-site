About Nagpur · Bharat

Kamlesh Bhagatkar

Politician, Author & Geopolitical Analyst

Kamlesh Bhagatkar is a politician, author and geopolitical analyst from Nagpur, Maharashtra. For the past ten years he has studied geopolitics, national security and India's strategic choices, and he writes about them in plain language, from an Indian point of view.

His first book, RSS: 100 Years of Selfless Service – Engineers of Glorious Bharat (2025), tells the story of the Sangh's first century for young readers. His next book, Nineteen Days: Pahalgam, Operation Sindoor and the Answer of Bharat, is a narrative account of India's response to the Pahalgam terror attack.

He publishes analysis on geopolitics and India's foreign and security policy on this site, and long-form research as whitepapers. Alongside his writing, he runs an IT business with offices in Nagpur and Bengaluru.
