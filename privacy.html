Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026

kamleshbhagatkar.com is a personal website. It has no accounts, no forms, no comments, no cookies and no advertising or tracking scripts. It does not collect or store personal data.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

The site's own publishing tool posts new articles from this website to the author's own accounts on other sites (for example Blogger and LinkedIn). It only publishes the author's articles and does not read or collect data about anyone.

Data deletion. The publishing tool stores no data about anyone, so there is nothing to delete. To remove its access to an account, revoke the "Kamlesh Site Poster" app in that site's settings, or email the address below.
